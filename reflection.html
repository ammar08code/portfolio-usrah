MY REFLECTION

Wish & Reflection

Usrah gave me opportunities to learn, participate and reflect on my experiences.

01

My Future Goals

In the future, I hope to continue improving my knowledge and skills in engineering, technology and programming. I want to become someone who can use knowledge and technology to create useful solutions and contribute positively to society.

02

What I Gained From Usrah

Throughout Usrah, I gained a better understanding of the importance of teamwork, communication, responsibility and reflection. The activities also encouraged me to appreciate Islamic values and apply them in my daily life.

03

How I Feel After Completing the Tasks

After completing all the tasks, I feel satisfied because I was able to finish the activities together with my group members. The experience also gave me a sense of accomplishment and helped me become more confident in working as part of a team.

04

My Suggestion for Improvement

One improvement I would suggest for Usrah is to include more interactive activities and discussions. This could encourage students to participate more actively and make the learning experience more engaging.
